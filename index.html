About Me

I am a seasoned roboticist with over 7 years of experience in real-time computer vision, machine learning, and robotics. My expertise spans across autonomous systems including passenger vehicles, trucks, and advanced humanoid robots such as Boston Dynamics' ATLAS. With a strong research background in robotics and computer vision, I have a proven track record of publishing in leading journals and conferences.

My research focuses on advancing 3D object detection, semantic reasoning, and scene reconstruction, addressing complex challenges in perception for autonomous systems. I am proficient in Python, C/C++, CUDA, and modern machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, and TensorRT, with extensive experience deploying models on embedded platforms including Nvidia Drive Orin, AGX Xavier, and PX2.
